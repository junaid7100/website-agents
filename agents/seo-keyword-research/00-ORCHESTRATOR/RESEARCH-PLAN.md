# Research Plan

## Objective

Build a defensible keyword universe, keyword intelligence layer, page architecture, page briefs, and copywriting handoff.

## Planned stages

1. Business/project setup, business profiling, research strategy, business-relevance policy
2. Market and seed development (structured seed families for active modules)
3. Google research (raw + candidates)
4. Semrush research (raw + candidates)
5. Competitor / market research (business-appropriate competitor types)
6. Keyword intelligence: merge, dedupe, intent, cluster, SERP-validate, primary/secondary
7. Keyword-to-page mapping and page architecture (page types appropriate to the business)
8. Copywriting / content handoff (page-type-appropriate briefs)
9. Post-launch Search Console / GA4 measurement loop

## Enabled modules

Set from `research-strategy.json`; this plan lists only what the strategy enables. Modules not listed here are `DISABLED` or `CONDITIONAL`.

## Parallel stages

After Agent 01, Agents 02, 03, and 04 may run independently.

## Checkpoints

- Before 01: profile, strategy and relevance policy approved; open confirmation items reported.
- Gate D before 05 (candidate datasets present); Gate E before 06; Gate F before 07.
- Semrush: the Semrush stage asks for the access path itself at each use (per the Semrush policy in CLAUDE.md); the orchestrator does not ask or pre-decide it.
- Google Keyword Planner: verify location, language, and currency before every keyword-entry batch.
- Research completion: do not finalize keyword intelligence until Google, Semrush, and competitor/local inputs are complete, or the user has explicitly chosen to drop one.
