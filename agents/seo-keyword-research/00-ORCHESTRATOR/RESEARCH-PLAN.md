# Research Plan

## Objective

Build a defensible keyword universe, keyword intelligence layer, page architecture, page briefs, and copywriting handoff.

## Planned stages

1. Business/project setup
2. Market and seed development
3. Google research
4. Semrush research
5. Competitor and local SEO research
6. Keyword cleaning, intent, clustering, prioritization
7. Keyword-to-page mapping and page architecture
8. Copywriting handoff
9. Post-launch Search Console / GA4 measurement loop

## Parallel stages

After Agent 01, Agents 02, 03, and 04 may run independently.

## Checkpoints

- Semrush: choose MCP/API, internal browser, or manual export before each Semrush use (asked every time).
- Google Keyword Planner: verify location, language, and currency before every keyword-entry batch.
- Research completion: do not finalize keyword intelligence until Google, Semrush, and competitor/local inputs are complete or explicitly unavailable.
